History

From Beruwala to the trade.

Pure Avi Gems is based in Beruwala, Sri Lanka, and we have been doing business for years — a coastal town where the gem trade and the sea have long sat side by side.

Who we are

Pure Avi Gems trades as the partnership G.H.S Enterprises, exporting rough Ceylon sapphire and garnet parcels from Sri Lanka for cutters and dealers. Our base is Beruwala, on the south-west coast, where gem dealing has been part of daily life for generations.

Beruwala

Beruwala is a coastal town in Kalutara District, Western Province, Sri Lanka — known for its beach and as a popular holiday destination, as well as for harbour life and long-standing trade. Visitors come for the shore; those in the stone trade know it as one of the country’s established gem-market towns, where dealers, cutters, and brokers have long met to sort and move sapphires and other Ceylon material. That mix of sea, town, and trade is the backdrop for how Pure Avi Gems works: careful parcel work, direct conversation with buyers, and a pace that respects how this coast has done business for years.

Years in the trade

We have been doing business for years — building relationships with cutters and dealers, learning parcels by hand, and shipping Ceylon sapphire and garnet with the same care we expect when we buy. The partnership G.H.S Enterprises holds business registration certificate WP/COL/DP/2026/00281 (Western Province). Registered address: Mahasinghe Road, Hohandara East, Sri Lanka — while our trading base remains Beruwala.

Licence

Gem dealer licence (NGJA) No. 20259DL34127 under G.D.G. Harischandra. Nature of business: imports, export, buying and selling of gems. Authorised by the National Gem & Jewellery Authority of Sri Lanka.

What we ship

Buyers are cutters and dealers who take parcels. Material offered is Ceylon sapphire and garnet parcels sourced in Sri Lanka — colours, qualities, treatment, and parcel make-up are confirmed on inquiry. This site does not list live stock or prices.
