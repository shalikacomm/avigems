Who we are

Pure Avi Gems trades as the partnership G.H.S Enterprises, exporting rough Ceylon sapphire and garnet parcels and cut gemstones from Sri Lanka for cutters and dealers. Our trading bases are Ratnapura and Beruwala, where gem dealing has been part of daily life for generations.

Ratnapura

Ratnapura sits in Sabaragamuwa Province, Sri Lanka — long known as the country’s gem city, with mining country, dealers, cutters, and brokers meeting to sort and move sapphires and other Ceylon material. That inland setting is one half of how Pure Avi Gems works: careful parcel work grounded in the mining and dealing heartland.

Beruwala

Beruwala is a coastal town in Kalutara District, Western Province, Sri Lanka — known for its beach and as a popular holiday destination, as well as for harbour life and long-standing trade. Those in the stone trade know it as one of the country’s established gem-market towns, where dealers, cutters, and brokers have long met to sort and move sapphires and other Ceylon material. Together with Ratnapura, that mix of mining country and coast is the backdrop for how Pure Avi Gems works: careful parcel work, direct conversation with buyers, and a pace that respects how this trade has been done for years.

Years in the trade

We have been doing business for years — building relationships with cutters and dealers, learning parcels by hand, and shipping rough and cut Ceylon sapphire and garnet with the same care we expect when we buy. The partnership G.H.S Enterprises holds business registration certificate WP/COL/DP/2026/00281 (Western Province). Registered address: Mahasinghe Road, Hohandara East, Sri Lanka — while our trading bases remain Ratnapura and Beruwala.

Licence

Gem dealer licence (NGJA) No. 20259DL34127 under G.D.G. Harischandra. Nature of business: imports, export, buying and selling of gems. Authorised by the National Gem & Jewellery Authority of Sri Lanka.

What we ship

Buyers are cutters and dealers. Material offered includes rough Ceylon sapphire and garnet parcels and cut gemstones sourced in Sri Lanka — colours, qualities, treatment, and make-up are confirmed on inquiry. This site does not list live stock or prices.
