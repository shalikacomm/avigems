History

From Beruwala to the trade.

Pure Avi Gems is based in Beruwala — a coastal town long tied to Sri Lanka’s gem trade — and we have been doing business for years.

Who we are

Pure Avi Gems trades as the partnership G.H.S Enterprises, exporting rough Ceylon sapphire and garnet parcels from Sri Lanka for cutters and dealers. Our base is Beruwala, on the south-west coast, where gem dealing has been part of daily life for generations.

Beruwala

Beruwala sits in Kalutara District, Sri Lanka, on the Indian Ocean. It is known for its harbour, fishing community, and — for those in the stone trade — as one of the country’s established gem-market towns. Dealers, cutters, and brokers have long met here to sort and move sapphires and other Ceylon material. That setting is the backdrop for how Pure Avi Gems works: careful parcel work, direct conversation with buyers, and a pace that respects how this trade has been done for years.

Years in the trade

We have been doing business for years — building relationships with cutters and dealers, learning parcels by hand, and shipping Ceylon sapphire and garnet with the same care we expect when we buy. The partnership G.H.S Enterprises was formally commenced on 19-02-2026 and registered on 04-03-2026 (certificate WP/COL/DP/2026/00281, Western Province), with a registered address at No: 318/K, 1st Lane, Malasinghegoda Road, Hokandara Eastern, Sri Lanka — while our trading base remains Beruwala.

What we ship

Buyers are cutters and dealers who take parcels. Material offered is Ceylon sapphire and garnet parcels sourced in Sri Lanka — colours, qualities, treatment, and parcel make-up are confirmed on inquiry. This site does not list live stock or prices.
