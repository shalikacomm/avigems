History

From Sri Lanka to the trade.

A short note on how Pure Avi Gems began and how parcels are offered.

Who we are

Pure Avi Gems trades as the partnership G.H.S Enterprises, exporting rough Ceylon sapphire and garnet parcels from Sri Lanka for cutters and dealers.

When and where

The partnership commenced on 19-02-2026 and was registered on 04-03-2026 (certificate WP/COL/DP/2026/00281, Western Province). The business address is No: 318/K, 1st Lane, Malasinghegoda Road, Hokandara Eastern, Sri Lanka.

Licence

Gem dealer licence (NGJA) No. 20259DL34127 under G.D.G. Harischandra (NIC 600011219V), Bronze tier, stock value Rs. 500,000. Nature of business: imports, export, buying and selling of gems. Capital Rs. 500,000. Authorised by the National Gem & Jewellery Authority of Sri Lanka.

What we ship

Buyers are cutters and dealers who take parcels. Material offered is Ceylon sapphire and garnet parcels sourced in Sri Lanka — colours, qualities, treatment, and parcel make-up are confirmed on inquiry. This site does not list live stock or prices.
