Safe & Legal Export
Every gem shipped
the right way

We export exclusively through Sri Lanka's National Gem & Jewellery Authority (NGJA) — the official government body that certifies and approves all gem exports from Sri Lanka. Every stone comes with official NGJA documentation, is 100% legal, and is fully insured in transit to your Australian address.

01
NGJA Gem Export Certificate
Every shipment carries an official Sri Lanka NGJA export certificate — proof of legal origin, gem identity, and government approval before leaving Sri Lanka.
02
Fully insured in transit
Each parcel is insured for its full declared value from Colombo all the way to your Australian address. You are completely protected if anything goes wrong.
03
Australian customs cleared
All import duties and GST are handled correctly. You receive a clean, duty-paid parcel with no surprise fees at your door.
04
Tracked express delivery
Shipped via DHL or FedEx insured express with full tracking. Typical delivery 5–9 business days to any Australian address.
National Gem & Jewellery Authority · Government of Sri Lanka
Government-certified gem exports
The NGJA is the Sri Lankan government authority responsible for regulating and certifying all gem and jewellery exports. Every stone we sell carries an NGJA export certificate — internationally recognised proof of legal origin and authenticity. You can verify any certificate at the NGJA's official registry.
